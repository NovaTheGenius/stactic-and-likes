How it works

The honest version of the process

No jargon, no “we’ll handle everything” promises we can’t keep. Here’s what actually happens when you work with us.

01

Tell us your story

Your grades, your budget (be honest, we don't judge), your dream country. One 15-minute call or a few WhatsApp messages is enough.

02

We shortlist real options

No copy-paste lists. Every school we suggest fits your budget and your transcript, and we tell you upfront when something is a reach.

03

We apply together

We review your statement of purpose, chase transcripts, double-check every deadline, and submit. You stay in the loop the whole way.

04

You fly out

Visa interview prep (we rehearse the tough questions), accommodation guidance, and someone picking you up where we can arrange it.

What we do

More than just applications

🔍

Arrival & settling in
